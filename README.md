# 💪 ABS Workout Tracker

A simple, interactive, and responsive web application to track your daily ab workouts, monitor progress, and stay consistent with your fitness goals.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://adnuhan.github.io/ABS-Workout-Tracker/

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Track daily ab workouts** – Log exercises with sets, reps, and duration
- 📊 **Progress visualization** – See your weekly and monthly progress at a glance
- 🔥 **Streak counter** – Maintain motivation with consecutive workout tracking
- 📅 **Calendar view** – Visual representation of your workout history
- 💪 **Pre-set ab exercises** – Crunches, Leg Raises, Planks, Russian Twists, and more
- ✏️ **Custom exercise addition** – Add your own ab workout routines
- ⏱️ **Rest timer** – Built-in timer for rest periods between sets
- 📈 **Statistics dashboard** – Total workouts, most consistent days, favorite exercises
- 🎯 **Goal setting** – Set weekly workout targets and track achievement
- 🎨 **Clean, modern UI** – Designed for easy use during workouts
- 📱 **Fully responsive** – Works perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- 💾 **Local storage** – Your workout data is saved locally in your browser
- 🚫 **No account needed** – Start tracking immediately without signup
- 🌐 **Zero dependencies** – Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** – Structure and semantic markup
- **CSS3** – Modern styling, flexbox, grid, and animations
- **Vanilla JavaScript** – No frameworks, just pure JS for logic and DOM manipulation
- **Local Storage API** – Persistent data saving
- **GitHub Pages** – Deployment and hosting
